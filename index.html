Sources and method
  • Base wage default: BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025, cargo and freight agents (SOC 43-5011), national mean annual wage $57,230. Dispatchers (43-5032) mean is $54,740 for comparison.
  • Loaded-cost multiplier 1.46: BLS Employer Costs for Employee Compensation, June 2026. Wages are 68.5% of total compensation for full-time private industry workers, so 1 / 0.685.
  • Productive hours 1,880: starting assumption, 2,080 paid hours less about 200 hours of leave and holidays.
  • Workflow touches and minutes: starting assumptions per segment, to be replaced with the prospect's own numbers.
  • FX: European Central Bank reference rates via the Frankfurter API, fetched live.
  • Method: hours freed = loads × 12 × minutes per load ÷ 60 × share automated. Value = hours freed × loaded hourly cost, capped at the team's total loaded cost. Freed hours are capacity, not a headcount cut.
