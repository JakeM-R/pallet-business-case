# Business case builder

One-page business case tool for supply chain AI deals: enter a prospect's volume, manual workflows, team and quoted price, get payback in low/base/high cases, the automation share and price needed for 12-month payback, and a PDF one-pager.

Live: https://jakem-r.github.io/pallet-business-case/
